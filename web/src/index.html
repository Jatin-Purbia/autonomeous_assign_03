The reasoning pipeline

Conflict-free: no attack has both endpoints in S.

Defence: S attacks every attacker of the argument. No attackers means automatic defence.

Characteristic function F(S): all arguments defended by S.

Admissible: conflict-free and S ⊆ F(S). Complete: conflict-free and S = F(S).

Grounded: iterate F from ∅ until unchanged. Preferred: inclusion-maximal complete sets.

Stable: conflict-free and attacks all outside arguments. Semi-stable: complete with inclusion-maximal range S ∪ S⁺.

Two separate checks

Theorem 5.3 exhaustively checks truthfulness against every combination of other reports. Theorem 5.4's structural condition is sufficient; its failure alone does not establish manipulation.

The parity search explores at most 2|A| states per source. It allows repeated vertices and includes odd self-return walks. This conservative cycle convention is recorded with every result.
